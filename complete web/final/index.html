About Our Girls PG Hostel

The Grand Riyasat Luxury Girls PG Hostel is designed to provide a safe, comfortable, and supportive living environment for students and working women. We understand the importance of security and peace of mind, which is why our premises are equipped with 24×7 CCTV surveillance, trained security guards, and lady bouncers. Our hostel offers fully air-conditioned rooms, modern furnishings, clean washrooms, and high-speed Wi-Fi connectivity to support both study and work requirements.

Residents enjoy nutritious breakfast, lunch, and dinner prepared in hygienic kitchens. We focus on maintaining cleanliness throughout the property through regular housekeeping services. Located near educational institutions, offices, markets, and public transport facilities, our hostel ensures convenience and accessibility.

We believe in creating a home-like atmosphere where girls can feel comfortable, confident, and independent. The hostel management is professional, responsive, and committed to providing quality services. Our affordable pricing and premium amenities make us one of the best accommodation options for girls seeking a secure and pleasant stay.
